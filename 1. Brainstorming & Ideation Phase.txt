1. Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

Project Name: EduGenie – Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

The idea of EduGenie is to develop an AI-powered educational assistant that helps students learn concepts easily and interactively.

The main idea is to use Generative AI to provide:
• Smart question and answer
• Simplified explanations for complex concepts
• Quiz generation
• Educational text summarization
• Personalized learning recommendations

The system is designed for students, self-learners, and educational users. EduGenie aims to make learning easier, faster, and more personalized through Artificial Intelligence.
